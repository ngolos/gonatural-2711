# Go Natural — Creative Workspace Design

## Visual direction
Use the uploaded studio website as inspiration only: white/near-black sections, quiet navigation, very large sans-serif headings, a small serif wordmark, straight edges and broad whitespace. No reference copy, portraits or brand assets are reused.

Desktop: 226px dark sidebar, off-white working surface, fine gray borders. Broad editorial headings and a dark context panel. Square/4px paper panels, not pill-shaped cards. No gradients, fake analytics or invented branded bottle imagery.

## Design tokens
Background #f7f7f2; paper #ffffff; charcoal #171917; ink #252824; muted #757970; border #dedfd6; olive #6b7755. Status green #426a50, amber #986c2b, red #a3483e with pale matching surfaces. Helvetica/Arial body and display, Georgia editorial wordmark. Display 46–56px regular; section 24–30px; body 14px/1.6; metadata 11–12px. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64px.

## Interaction and responsive rules
Persistent scope breadcrumb and sample label. Accessible icon-plus-text actions, explicit busy/error states, clear approval/request-change actions. Missing references have large intentional neutral panels labeled Not uploaded. Review uses large image viewer, normalized pins, version selector and comparison. Mobile collapses sidebar into compact horizontal navigation and stacks columns. Visible keyboard focus and generous click targets.

## Data honesty
Imported texts retain source filenames and import date. Historical creator feedback is not client approval. Sample products and staged decisions are labeled. No image generation in this build; Four supplied boards and eight draft executions are stored privately. Actual SKU identity references remain absent.

## Screens
Overview, Campaigns, Products/detail, Creative Library, Approved Assets, Review, Production packet, Import/export and Product intake. Typed oRPC procedures and database-backed access controls; web package only.

## Portfolio correction
One named fragrance/product identity per card, all documented non-bundle types as compact rectangular badges. Workbook family colors are used only for small family markers, not invented packaging. Fragrance detail opens with available-format tiles and attributed sizes/variant labels. Preserve raw matrix evidence and visibly flag inconsistent classifications; no invented sizes or variant IDs. Hide fictional navigation records from portfolio without deleting historical links. Keep family and type filters composable, sorting and navigation state persistent, with a clear reset action.
